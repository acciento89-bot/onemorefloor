# ONE MORE FLOOR v1.43 — Ossuary 3D Rollout

v1.43 extends the production 3D gameplay presentation from floors 1–10 into the Ossuary, floors 11–20.

## Included

- production 3D bridge now active through floor 20
- automatic realm switch at floor 11
- dedicated Ossuary environment root and visual identity
- side crypt bays with stone frames and bone shelves
- sarcophagi, bone altar, necromantic focus, bone pylons and iron floor channels
- cold teal / necromantic violet lighting distinct from Lower Halls
- animated ritual focus and floor sigils
- Lower Halls production props/lights are hidden while the Ossuary kit is active
- existing v1.42 rigged-model pipeline remains active for Skeleton, Ghoul, Necromancer and other actor slots
- dedicated v1.43 smoke test and CI

## Runtime ownership

Combat math, AI, hitboxes, drops, XP, coins, saves, progression, touch controls and run state remain unchanged. The 3D layer continues to mirror the tested gameplay runtime.

## Realm coverage

- Floors 1–10: Lower Halls 3D
- Floors 11–20: Ossuary 3D
- Floors 21+: stable legacy fallback until the next 3D realm kit is migrated

## Next milestone

The next environment migration is the Iron Bastion for floors 21–30. Production GLB character files can also be introduced at any point now that v1.42 established the rig/import contract.
