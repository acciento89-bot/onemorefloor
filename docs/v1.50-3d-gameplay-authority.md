# ONE MORE FLOOR v1.50 — 3D Gameplay Authority Phase 1

v1.50 is the first release that moves a real piece of gameplay authority out of the historical 2D runtime and into the 3D world. The visual bridge from v1.39–v1.49 remains intact, but final locomotion endpoints on the original floors 1–50 are now resolved by real `CharacterBody3D` nodes inside the same 3D world used for presentation.

## What becomes 3D-authoritative

The 2D runtime still computes player input intent, enemy AI intent, attack timing, damage, projectiles, rewards, XP, economy and progression. After those systems calculate desired positions, v1.50 routes the resulting player/enemy endpoints into the new `world3d_gameplay_authority_v150.gd` bridge.

The bridge owns:

- one persistent player `CharacterBody3D`,
- eighteen pooled enemy `CharacterBody3D` nodes,
- four `StaticBody3D` arena bounds,
- per-actor `CapsuleShape3D` collision shapes,
- design-space ↔ world-space mapping,
- final arena-bound correction,
- world-space enemy/enemy de-overlap,
- persistent body motion between frames,
- 3D contact-pressure reporting,
- correction/wall/separation instrumentation.

The corrected positions are written back into the existing runtime before the frame is rendered and become the positions used by the next gameplay frame.

## Why this is hybrid instead of a risky full rewrite

The existing combat loop is already heavily tested and contains years of accumulated progression, boss, economy, touch and live-ops behavior. Moving damage, projectile simulation, AI decision making and all collision rules into 3D in one release would make regressions much harder to isolate.

v1.50 therefore moves the most structural part first: locomotion/collision finalization. This establishes persistent physics bodies and static world bounds while leaving combat math untouched.

The mode is reported as:

`hybrid_3d_collision_authority`

## Compatibility behavior

Player/enemy overlap is intentionally not hard-blocked yet. Enemy bodies are separated from each other in world space, and all actors are constrained by the 3D arena. Player/enemy proximity is measured and reported as a 3D contact pair, but touch damage still uses the existing runtime logic in v1.50. This preserves current combat balance while preparing a clean future migration to `Area3D`/shape-based contact damage.

## CharacterBody3D pool

The authority layer preallocates nineteen gameplay bodies:

- 1 Wanderer body,
- 18 enemy bodies.

Enemy shapes update their radius from the runtime enemy radius. Bodies are reused instead of spawned/despawned during combat, matching the mobile pooling discipline already used for presentation VFX.

## Arena authority

Four `StaticBody3D` walls surround the mapped arena. Final body endpoints are additionally clamped against world bounds using their actual 3D collision radius. This gives deterministic behavior even during headless CI while retaining real Godot collision objects and `move_and_collide()` motion for persistent bodies.

Large discontinuities, such as a new floor spawning actors far from their prior positions, use a controlled teleport reset. Normal frame-to-frame motion stays persistent.

## Enemy separation

The previous 2D runtime allowed enemies to stack tightly because their movement only clamped to the arena. v1.50 performs a bounded pairwise world-space separation pass after movement intent has been applied. With at most eighteen enemies this is a small, deterministic workload and prevents the most obvious body stacking without changing enemy AI decisions.

## 3D contact pressure

The authority bridge measures player/enemy proximity using the real world-space radii and exposes `contact_pairs` in its report. The value is diagnostic in v1.50; it does not apply damage yet.

This is the planned hand-off point for the next migration step, where touch/contact damage can move from distance math to `Area3D`/shape overlap without changing the rest of combat.

## Runtime integration

`scripts/main_v63.gd` extends the v1.49 runtime and runs the authority finalizer after the existing gameplay intent update on floors 1–50.

The resulting positions are copied back into:

- `player_pos`,
- each enemy dictionary's `pos`.

The v1.50 3D chamber then renders those corrected positions with all existing v1.46–v1.49 camera, boss, art, lighting and combat presentation systems.

## Authority feedback

`world3d_chamber_v150.gd` adds a fixed ten-slot pooled authority-impact layer. Corrections and world-space collision/separation points can produce subtle floor rings so physics pressure is readable without adding heavy particle systems.

## Instrumentation

The authority report exposes:

- frame number,
- active enemy bodies,
- wall hits,
- enemy separation hits,
- player/enemy contact pairs,
- corrected body count,
- maximum design-space correction,
- collision points,
- player world-space motion.

`main_v63.gd` accumulates the key counts for telemetry/debug snapshots.

## What remains intentionally unchanged

v1.50 does **not** claim that the entire game has moved to 3D physics. The following remain under the tested runtime:

- enemy AI decision logic,
- auto-attack targeting,
- projectile movement/hits,
- touch/contact damage,
- Warden cast logic,
- damage formulas,
- XP and rewards,
- coins/economy,
- progression and saves,
- touch controls,
- 2D HUD/menu authority.

The original floors 1–50 use the new 3D locomotion/collision finalizer. Other/endless content outside that original tower is not automatically claimed as migrated.

## Production assets

The v1.47–v1.49 GLB/GLTF import and asset-quality pipeline remains active. v1.50 does not fabricate binary character models.

## Mobile discipline

- fixed 19 CharacterBody3D pool,
- fixed 4 StaticBody3D arena bounds,
- fixed 10 authority-feedback slots,
- no per-frame scene spawning,
- no SDFGI or volumetric dependency,
- no dynamic shadow expansion,
- pairwise separation bounded to 18 enemies and two passes.

## CI

`v63_3d_gameplay_authority_smoke_test.gd` verifies:

- 19 CharacterBody3D nodes,
- four static bounds,
- out-of-bounds correction,
- overlapping enemy separation,
- persistent authority frame advancement,
- chamber integration,
- authority feedback pool,
- main-scene v1.50 readiness.

The dedicated workflow runs on Godot 4.7.1 and is supplemented by the existing full Godot regression and iOS playtest workflows.
