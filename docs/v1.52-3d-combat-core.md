# ONE MORE FLOOR v1.52 — 3D Combat Core Phase 3

v1.52 continues the controlled migration of gameplay authority into the real 3D tower. v1.50 moved final locomotion and arena collision to `CharacterBody3D`. v1.51 moved touch triggering and projectile travel/hit selection to 3D. v1.52 now moves **auto-attack target range, NOVA volume selection and Warden cast geometry** into world-space authority on the original floors 1–50.

The release remains deliberately hybrid: combat geometry keeps moving into 3D while the already-tested balance math, progression and live gameplay rules remain stable.

## What becomes 3D-authoritative

### Auto-attack targeting

`fire_auto_attack()` still owns attack cadence, multishot count, crit rolls, damage payload and projectile creation. Its target lookup now dynamically dispatches into `world3d_combat_query_authority_v152.gd`.

The new authority owns:

- a real `Area3D` attack-range sensor,
- a `SphereShape3D` matching the active attack range,
- design-space → tower-world conversion,
- world-space distance filtering,
- world-space nearest-target ordering,
- ignore-list handling for multishot target selection.

The returned enemy index is the target that survives the frame as the attack choice.

Mode marker: `3d_world_range`.

### NOVA

The skill button, cooldown, animation flash, haptics, damage formula and effects remain in the runtime. The question **which enemies and hostile projectiles are inside NOVA** is now answered by a real 3D spherical combat volume.

The new NOVA authority owns:

- one persistent `Area3D`,
- one persistent `SphereShape3D`,
- the world-space center,
- the world-space radius,
- enemy membership,
- hostile projectile membership.

The runtime then applies the existing NOVA damage to the returned enemy indices and removes only the returned hostile projectile indices.

Mode marker: `3d_nova_volume`.

### Warden cast geometry

Warden AI still decides **when** to cast and whether the cast is `fan` or `ring`. v1.52 moves the geometry of that cast into the 3D tower.

The authority owns:

- world-space aim from Warden to Wanderer,
- seven persistent `Area3D` fan threat lanes,
- one persistent Warden focus sensor,
- phase-aware ring direction generation,
- seven fan launch directions,
- ten phase-1 ring launch directions,
- fourteen phase-2 ring launch directions,
- world-space lane/player intersection reporting for telemetry and presentation.

Those 3D directions are converted back into the existing projectile payloads. v1.51 then owns projectile travel and hit selection in 3D.

Mode marker: `3d_warden_cast_geometry`.

## Visible 3D authority feedback

v1.52 also makes the new query layer visible without adding gameplay allocations:

- four pooled target-lock markers,
- one persistent NOVA boundary/core visual,
- seven pooled Warden fan-lane visuals,
- one Warden ring-threat visual.

These are presentation only. They do not add damage and do not decide balance.

## Combined authority stack after v1.52

On floors 1–50, the following decisions now have real 3D authority:

1. final player locomotion endpoint,
2. final enemy locomotion endpoints,
3. arena-bound collision,
4. enemy/enemy separation,
5. player/enemy contact trigger,
6. player projectile travel and hit selection,
7. enemy projectile travel and hit selection,
8. auto-attack range and nearest-target selection,
9. NOVA enemy/projectile volume membership,
10. Warden ring/fan cast geometry.

The runtime still owns:

- player input intent,
- enemy AI state decisions,
- attack cadence,
- crit chance and damage multipliers,
- HP and armor math,
- lifesteal,
- Warden cast timing/choice,
- XP/rewards/coins,
- upgrades,
- saves,
- progression,
- economy/liveops,
- touch controls,
- 2D HUD and menu stack.

Overall mode marker: `hybrid_3d_combat_core_authority`.

## Mobile/performance discipline

The new combat-core authority is allocation-stable during gameplay:

- attack, NOVA and Warden sensors are persistent,
- Warden lanes are preallocated,
- target feedback is pooled,
- NOVA and Warden feedback nodes are persistent,
- no per-frame mesh creation,
- no per-enemy dynamic lights,
- no new shadow casters,
- no asynchronous physics-signal dependency for combat decisions.
