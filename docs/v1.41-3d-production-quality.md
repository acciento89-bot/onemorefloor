# ONE MORE FLOOR v1.41 — 3D Production Quality Pass

This pass keeps the existing gameplay runtime authoritative and pushes the Lower Halls pilot toward a production-ready mobile 3D presentation.

## Included

- production actor extension for the Wanderer and early-floor enemies
- stronger armor/cape/weapon detail and secondary movement
- real 3D enemy attack tells and hit flashes
- contact-shadow geometry under actors for stronger grounding
- projectile trail geometry and pooled impact flashes
- improved light composition and conservative color grading
- denser Lower Halls architecture with buttresses, braziers, floor sigils and rubble
- dedicated v1.41 smoke test and CI

## Intentionally unchanged

Combat math, enemy AI, collisions, rewards, economy, save data, touch geometry and floor progression remain owned by the tested runtime. Floors 1-10 continue to be the 3D pilot; later realms still use the stable fallback until their own environment kits are migrated.

## Next visual milestone

After this pass is stable, the next step is replacing native primitive-built characters with imported rigged production meshes/animations and then migrating the Ossuary (floors 11-20) to its own 3D environment kit.
