# ONE MORE FLOOR v1.46 — Production Vertical Slice

v1.46 keeps the complete five-realm 3D tower from v1.45 and upgrades the combat presentation layer without moving gameplay authority out of the proven runtime.

## Scope

- Floors 1–50 remain in production 3D.
- Existing imported/rigged GLB/GLTF model fallback pipeline remains active.
- Existing combat math, collisions, enemy AI, rewards, economy, progression, saves and touch controls remain authoritative and unchanged.
- Presentation is layered on top of the current SubViewport/Node3D bridge so the 2D HUD and menus remain stable.

## Presentation upgrades

### Camera choreography

The orthographic gameplay camera keeps the tested arena framing, but now receives restrained production motion:

- short zoom/push on floor changes,
- stronger realm-boundary push on floors 1, 11, 21, 31 and 41,
- boss-floor emphasis on floors 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50,
- tiny pooled camera kick for attack/skill events.

The movement is intentionally small so visual presentation improves without breaking the legacy 2D-to-3D coordinate bridge.

### Realm transitions

A pooled 3D transition layer now masks hard realm swaps with realm-colored emissive rings, runes and a temporary local light. Colors follow the active realm: Lower Halls gold, Ossuary teal, Iron Bastion orange, Rift Descent violet and Starless Spire blue.

### Boss framing

Boss floors receive an in-world presentation frame around the strongest/warden enemy:

- floor halo,
- vertical accent beam,
- animated crown markers,
- local non-shadow accent light,
- short camera emphasis on boss-floor entry.

### Combat telegraphs

A fixed pool of 18 ground telegraphs mirrors the runtime's existing attack cooldown/tell data. Elite and Warden attacks use a stronger amber treatment while regular danger tells remain red. No collision or timing data is changed.

### Death feedback

A fixed pool of 12 death bursts compares successive mirrored enemy positions. When an enemy disappears, the presentation layer emits a short ring/shard burst at its last mirrored position. This is visual only and does not determine whether an enemy is dead.

### Loot presentation

The existing coin state now receives 24 pooled 3D loot markers with subtle vertical beams and floor glows. Larger coin values scale presentation slightly without changing pickup logic or reward values.

### Player attack/skill feedback

The Wanderer receives lightweight pooled floor pulses for attacks and skills. These are driven from the existing attack/skill flash values and do not add new gameplay events.

## Mobile discipline

- no volumetric fog or GI,
- no per-frame mesh allocation,
- fixed-size pools for telegraphs, death effects and loot markers,
- secondary lights do not cast shadows,
- orthographic camera retained,
- StandardMaterial3D/emissive primitives only.

## Next production step

The presentation bridge is now ready for higher-quality imported character assets and realm-specific VFX textures/materials. The next art-focused pass can replace procedural actor visuals model-by-model while keeping this production presentation layer intact.
