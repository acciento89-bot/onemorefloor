# ONE MORE FLOOR v1.48 — Character Combat VFX & Animation Presentation

v1.48 builds on the complete five-realm 3D tower, v1.46 production vertical slice and v1.47 actor production contract. The goal is more readable, more character-specific combat without moving any gameplay authority out of the tested runtime.

## Scope

- Floors 1–50 remain real 3D through the shared SubViewport bridge.
- The v1.47 GLB/GLTF import, socket and animation contract stays active.
- Combat math, enemy AI, collisions, rewards, XP, economy, progression, saves, touch controls and the 2D HUD/menu stack are unchanged.
- New effects are pooled, mesh-based and mobile-safe.

## Archetype combat language

Each mirrored enemy slot now has a fixed character-combat VFX rig. Effects are positioned from the stable production sockets instead of hard-coded screen-space coordinates.

- Goblin: weapon-socket slash arc and short charge core.
- Bat: head-rune pulse plus orbiting attack motes.
- Skeleton: weapon-socket attack arc with pale bone/gold energy.
- Ghoul: toxic chest/core pulse and close-range orbiting claw energy.
- Necromancer: persistent violet spell core, head rune and three orbiting spell motes.
- Warden: weapon arc, head rune, local accent light and a three-stage ground shockwave sequence.

The Warden's highest tell phase also adds a restrained camera impact. The trigger is presentation-only and never changes attack timing.

## Wanderer combat presentation

The Wanderer now gets socket-driven combat feedback layered on top of the v1.47 seven-segment weapon trail:

- weapon-tip flare during attacks and skills,
- expanding chest/ground sigil during NOVA/skill presentation,
- six-piece overhead skill crown,
- non-shadow local combat light,
- stronger attack/skill body motion from the v1.48 actor factory.

All of these follow the v1.47 production sockets, so future imported rigs can inherit the same presentation without rewriting combat code.

## Spawn and death signatures

v1.48 adds two fixed pools of 12 archetype-colored signatures. New mirrored enemies receive a short material-matched spawn collapse; disappearing enemies receive an expanding death signature. The existing v1.46 generic death feedback remains intact underneath this layer.

No actor disappearance or death state is decided by these effects. They only mirror changes already made by the authoritative runtime.

## Animation presentation

`world3d_actor_factory_v148.gd` extends the production actor factory with stronger archetype motion language and a hit one-shot bridge:

- goblin body twist,
- bat banking and dive posture,
- skeleton bracing,
- ghoul lunge posture,
- necromancer hover/rotation,
- Warden anticipation weight,
- Wanderer attack/skill body drive.

Imported models still receive the production animation states through the existing registry. Native fallbacks receive the extra presentation directly on their `Motion` roots.

## Mobile discipline

- 18 fixed enemy VFX slots; no runtime spawning per attack.
- 12 fixed spawn signatures and 12 fixed death signatures.
- no volumetric fog/GI requirement,
- secondary VFX lights do not cast shadows,
- StandardMaterial3D emissive primitives only,
- no gameplay physics migration,
- no per-frame mesh allocation in the new v1.48 layer.

## Next production step

The next largest quality jump is asset-driven: import the first real rigged Wanderer GLB and one production enemy model, validate skeleton/animation/socket mapping, then tune materials and silhouette against the now-complete camera/VFX stack.
