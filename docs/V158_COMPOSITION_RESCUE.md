# v1.58 Composition Rescue

This pass intentionally reduces procedural foreground geometry after the v1.57 TestFlight screenshots showed that additional blockout architecture made Home, Hero and Forge less readable.

## Non-negotiable framing rules

- Large meshes stay behind the presentation plane.
- Home and Hero keep the imported Wanderer fully readable in the open area above their main UI cards.
- Forge props stay on the rear wall and cannot clip the portrait edges.
- No angled side-wall slabs or near-camera castle/forge masses are allowed on the three rescued screens.
- Existing 2D UI, touch navigation, saves and gameplay authority remain unchanged.
