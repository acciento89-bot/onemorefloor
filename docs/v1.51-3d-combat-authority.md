# ONE MORE FLOOR v1.51 — 3D Combat Authority Phase 2

v1.51 moves two more gameplay decisions out of the historical 2D collision loop and into the real 3D tower: player/enemy touch triggering and projectile travel/hit selection on the original floors 1–50.

The release deliberately remains hybrid. v1.50 already made final player/enemy locomotion endpoints authoritative through persistent `CharacterBody3D` nodes and static 3D arena bounds. v1.51 keeps that architecture and adds real 3D contact reporting plus pooled `Area3D` projectile bodies and world-space sphere-sweep hit tests.

## What becomes 3D-authoritative

### Touch trigger

The legacy enemy AI still calculates movement intent. Before the old enemy update can apply its historical 2D distance-based touch trigger, v1.51 suppresses that trigger. After v1.50 resolves the final `CharacterBody3D` endpoints, the v1.51 contact authority reports the exact enemy indices touching the Wanderer in world space.

Those indices now decide **whether** touch damage occurs.

The existing runtime still decides:

- touch damage amount,
- armor reduction,
- HP mutation,
- knockback response,
- damage numbers,
- screen shake,
- haptics,
- death handling.

This keeps balance math stable while retiring the old 2D proximity check as the trigger source.

### Projectile travel and hit selection

Player and enemy projectile dictionaries still store gameplay payload such as damage, crit state, color and lifetime, but their movement/hit selection is no longer resolved by the old 2D point-distance loop on floors 1–50.

`world3d_projectile_authority_v151.gd` preallocates:

- 28 player projectile `Area3D` nodes,
- 36 enemy projectile `Area3D` nodes,
- one `SphereShape3D` per projectile body.

That is a fixed pool of **64 real Area3D projectile bodies**.

Every active projectile is mapped into tower world space. Travel for the current frame is represented by a 3D segment, and hit selection uses a continuous segment-vs-sphere sweep against mapped 3D actor radii. This prevents fast projectiles from relying on a single end-point distance test and gives the combat bridge deterministic tunneling-resistant hit selection.

The mode is reported as:

`3d_sphere_sweep`

## Why the projectile query is deterministic

The projectile authority intentionally does not depend on asynchronous `Area3D.body_entered` signal timing. The Area3D nodes are the persistent 3D projectile objects, while hit selection uses explicit world-space 3D sweep math in the same gameplay update that advances the projectile.

That has three benefits:

1. deterministic headless CI,
2. no one-frame signal lag between gameplay and rendering,
3. no node allocation or signal churn per shot.

## Contact authority

`world3d_gameplay_authority_v151.gd` extends the v1.50 CharacterBody3D authority and adds:

- `contact_indices`,
- `contact_points`,
- `touch_trigger_authority = "3d_contact"`,
- combat-authority mode reporting.

The runtime maintains the same 0.62-second per-enemy touch cooldown behavior in a dedicated v1.51 cooldown table.

## Combat feedback

`world3d_chamber_v151.gd` adds a fixed 16-slot combat-authority impact pool. Authoritative player projectile hits use a gold/amber treatment and enemy projectile hits use a red treatment. Critical player hits receive a stronger initial scale.

This is layered on top of the existing v1.41–v1.49 impact, telegraph, boss, lighting and actor presentation systems.

## Mobile discipline

The new combat authority is designed around fixed pools and simple math:

- 64 persistent projectile Area3D nodes,
- 16 persistent hit-feedback nodes,
- no per-shot scene instantiation,
- no per-hit particle-node creation,
- no volumetric effects,
- no GI migration,
- no skeleton work added by the authority layer,
- bounded actor/projectile capacities inherited from the existing mobile runtime.
